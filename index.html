Meet the owner

I’m Dalton Strunk, owner and lead pilot at SkyTek Aerial Solutions. I grew up farming near St. Leo, Kansas, always looking for ways to make our small operation more efficient. That, along with my love of technology and a father who has always worked in aviation, is how I found drones.

I started SkyTek to help farmers and producers in and around Cheney get more done with drone technology, whether that means buying the right aircraft or hiring us to spray. I also wanted to build something my kids would think is cool and want to be part of someday.

Whether you are buying a drone or hiring us to spray, I stand behind the work, and I will make sure you get straight answers.
